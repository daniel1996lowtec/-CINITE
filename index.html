📖 HOW TO PLAY

🏆 1. BATTLE ROYALE OBJECTIVE

Drop from the sky onto CI Pipeline Island, scavenge weapons and shield potions from Supply Chests across 8 Named POIs, stay inside the shrinking Safe Zone, and be the last Commander standing!

🔫 2. WEAPON RARITIES & COMBAT

Weapons scale across 5 tiers: Common (Grey), Uncommon (Green), Rare (Blue), Epic (Purple), and Legendary (Gold). Aim Down Sights (Right-Click) to tighten spread and recoil.

🧱 3. HARVESTING & TACTICAL BUILDING

Equip your CI Harvester (1) to harvest Wood, Stone, and Metal from trees, rocks, and crates. Press Q (Wall), E (Ramp), C (Floor), or V (Roof) to preview and place cover!

🎮 4. DESKTOP & MOBILE CONTROLS

Move: WASD • Sprint: Shift • Jump/Glide: Space • Loot/Chest: F • Reload: R • Mute: M • Switch Material: G. On mobile/tablet, move with the bottom-left joystick (push to the gold outer ring to sprint), drag the right side of the screen to look, and use the FIRE, ADS, JUMP, LOOT, LOAD, SWAP, MAT, and build buttons or tap a weapon hotbar slot.
