## Rights status / release gate

The repository does not contain the original download record or asset-specific
licence grant. A matching public example is **not proof of redistribution rights**.
The owner should verify the applicable Adobe/Mixamo terms and the original
acquisition before shipping this asset. 3.1 preserves the pre-existing asset;
it does not claim to resolve that legal provenance gap. If rights cannot be
confirmed, replace it with a documented licensed rig or ship the code-generated
fallback instead. No unverified replacement assets are introduced.

## Authored additions

Ready/Switch are short code-generated pose layers, not downloaded mocap.

repository code. Outfit plates use rigid bone-following transforms, **not skin
weights**. No external texture packs were added.
